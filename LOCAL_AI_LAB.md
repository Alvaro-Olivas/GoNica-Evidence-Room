# Local AI Lab

## Current documented workstation

- Intel Core i5-12400
- 32 GB RAM
- NVIDIA RTX 2060
- 6 GB VRAM
- ASRock B760M Pro RS/D4

## Local model experiment

A roughly 17 GB quantized Muse Glimmer 30B model was loaded and generated locally through llama.cpp/Vulkan.

Observed generation speed was approximately 1.3 tokens/sec.

## Why this matters

The local-model experiment is not the product. It is a measurement tool for the current compute ceiling.

The primary project remains GoNica Brain: company understanding, CRM migration, structured business memory, implementation planning, governance, testing, and controlled execution.

The hardware experiment provides a reproducible before/after workload for evaluating stronger compute.
